Yannik Ammann

I'm a mathematician and software engineer in Zürich. I build quantitative software, bring AI automation to customers, love good food, and take photographs when I travel.

I started out in web development, where I first worked with open-source software, an idea that has fascinated me ever since. Studying mathematics at ETH Zürich then drew me to statistical computing. For my master’s thesis with Roche I built Bayesian models for cancer research: they predict a patient’s survival from how their tumour responds to treatment. That code became JointSurvivalModels.jl, a statistics package. What I learned along the way is that if you can describe a problem on paper, you can also implement it in code.
